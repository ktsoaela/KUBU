About this starter

Three HTML files, one stylesheet and, if you want it, htmx. Copy the folder, rename the pages and replace the text.

What each part does

  • dist/kubu.min.css: every component, themed for dark and light.
  • htmx: hx-boost on <body> swaps #content and keeps the header.
  • Your server: return whole pages; htmx picks out the part it needs.
Does anything need JavaScript?

No. The menu is a checkbox, the question you just opened is <details>, and the form checks its own fields.

Contact Page

A contact form with forgiving validation: errors wait until you leave a field, say how to fix it, and Send stays dimmed until the required fields are valid.

Uses: Forms, FormGroups, Collapse, Card

Write to us

We answer within one working day.

Enter your name so we know who to reply to.

Enter an email address with an @ and a dot, like lerato@example.com.

Add an order number or a phone number

Order numbers have 4 digits, like 1042.

Tell us a little more: at least 20 characters.

Visit the workshop

Main North Road, Teyateyaneng. Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00.

Code 
<div class="container section grid">
        <form class="col-12 md:col-8 form-container--guarded" id="contact-preview" action="page-contact.html" method="get">
        <h2>Write to us</h2>
        <p>We answer within one working day.</p>
        <div class="form-group">
            <label class="form-label" for="cp-name">Your name <span class="form-label__hint">(required)</span></label>
            <input class="form-input" id="cp-name" name="name" type="text" autocomplete="name" required aria-describedby="cp-name-error">
            <p class="form-error form-error--live" id="cp-name-error">Enter your name so we know who to reply to.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="form-group">
            <label class="form-label" for="cp-email">Email <span class="form-label__hint">(required)</span></label>
            <input class="form-input" id="cp-email" name="email" type="email" autocomplete="email" required pattern=".+@.+\..+" placeholder="e.g. lerato@example.com" aria-describedby="cp-email-error">
            <p class="form-error form-error--live" id="cp-email-error">Enter an email address with an @ and a dot, like lerato@example.com.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="form-group">
            <label class="form-label" for="cp-topic">Topic</label>
            <select class="form-select" id="cp-topic" name="topic">
                <option>An order</option>
                <option>Wholesale</option>
                <option>Something else</option>
            </select>
        </div>
        <details class="collapse-details">
            <summary>Add an order number or a phone number</summary>
            <div class="form-group">
                <label class="form-label" for="cp-order">Order number</label>
                <input class="form-input" id="cp-order" name="order" type="text" inputmode="numeric" pattern="[0-9]{4}" placeholder="e.g. 1042" aria-describedby="cp-order-error">
                <p class="form-error form-error--live" id="cp-order-error">Order numbers have 4 digits, like 1042.</p>
            </div>
            <div class="form-group">
                <label class="form-label" for="cp-phone">Phone</label>
                <input class="form-input" id="cp-phone" name="phone" type="tel" autocomplete="tel">
            </div>
        </details>
        <div class="form-group section">
            <label class="form-label" for="cp-message">Message <span class="form-label__hint">(required, 20 characters or more)</span></label>
            <textarea class="form-textarea" id="cp-message" name="message" rows="5" required minlength="20" aria-describedby="cp-message-error"></textarea>
            <p class="form-error form-error--live" id="cp-message-error">Tell us a little more: at least 20 characters.</p>
        </div>
        <button class="form-submit" type="submit">Send message</button>
    </form>
    <aside class="col-12 md:col-4" aria-label="Other ways to reach us">
        <article class="card">
            <h3 class="card__title">Visit the workshop</h3>
            <div class="card__body"><p>Main North Road, Teyateyaneng. Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00.</p></div>
        </article>
    </aside>
</div>

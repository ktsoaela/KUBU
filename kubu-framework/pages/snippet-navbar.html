Component menu

Navbar

The site header: the logo first, the labelled menu after it, and a utility row. On a narrow screen the links fold behind a control that says "Menu".

File: css/snippets/Header.css, with the Navbar molecule and Announcements

Example

Make the window narrower than 768 pixels to see the Menu control. It is a checkbox, so it works with scripts off.

Free delivery in Maseru until 30 November.

Code 
<header class="header">
    <p class="announcement">Free delivery in Maseru until 30 November.</p>
    <div class="header__utility">
        <a href="page-contact.html">Help</a>
        <a href="page-about.html">About us</a>
    </div>
    <nav class="navbar" aria-label="Shop">
        <a class="logo" href="page-home.html"><img src="../img/hippo-white.svg" alt="" width="30" height="30"> Basotho Crafts</a>
        <input class="header__toggle" type="checkbox" id="sn-menu">
        <label class="header__menu" for="sn-menu">Menu</label>
        <ul class="navbar-nav">
            <li class="nav-item"><a class="nav-link" href="page-home.html" aria-current="page">Home</a></li>
            <li class="nav-item"><a class="nav-link" href="page-product.html">Products</a></li>
            <li class="nav-item"><a class="nav-link" href="page-about.html">About</a></li>
            <li class="nav-item"><a class="nav-link" href="page-contact.html">Contact</a></li>
        </ul>
    </nav>
</header>

Parts

ClassRole
header.headerBlock: the brand-coloured site header.
.header__utilityElement: a small row of secondary links.
.announcementOptional one-line message, from Announcements.
.navbar, .navbar-nav, .nav-linkThe menu, from the Navbar molecule.
.logoThe logo and name, linking home. Always first.
.header__toggle, .header__menuElement: the hidden checkbox and its "Menu" label for narrow screens.

Order

  1. Logo
  2. The labelled links, seven at most
  3. Tools such as the theme switch, at the end

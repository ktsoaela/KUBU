Modal

A native <dialog> built from a header, a body and a footer. The browser traps focus, closes it on Esc and draws the backdrop.

File: css/organisms/Modal.css

Example

The button opens the dialog with commandfor and command="show-modal": no script. Buttons in a form method="dialog" close it.

Delete invoice 1042?

The invoice and its payment history will be removed. This cannot be undone.
Code 
<button class="button button--danger" type="button" commandfor="delete-dialog" command="show-modal">Delete invoice 1042</button>
<dialog class="modal" id="delete-dialog" aria-labelledby="delete-dialog-title">
    <header class="modal__header">
        <h2 class="modal__title" id="delete-dialog-title">Delete invoice 1042?</h2>
        <form method="dialog"><button class="modal__close" aria-label="Close">&times;</button></form>
    </header>
    <div class="modal__body">
        <p>The invoice and its payment history will be removed. This cannot be undone.</p>
    </div>
    <form class="modal__footer" method="dialog">
        <button class="button button--outline" value="cancel">Keep invoice</button>
        <button class="button button--danger" value="delete">Delete invoice</button>
    </form>
</dialog>

CSS-only fallback with :target

For older browsers, the same parts inside a .modal-overlay open when the URL points at its id. The close link goes back to the button that opened it.

Code

    <div class="modal" role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-labelledby="terms-title">
        <header class="modal__header">
            <h2 class="modal__title" id="terms-title">Terms of sale</h2>
            <a class="modal__close" href="#terms-open" aria-label="Close">&times;</a>
        </header>
        <div class="modal__body"><p>Returns are free within 30 days. Refunds reach your card in five working days.</p></div>
        <footer class="modal__footer"><a class="button button--primary" href="#terms-open">I have read the terms</a></footer>
    </div>
</div>

Parts

ClassRole
dialog.modalBlock: the dialog surface, with a soft shadow and a lightly blurred backdrop.
.modal__headerElement: the title row, with the close control in the corner.
.modal__titleElement: the heading. Point aria-labelledby at it.
.modal__closeElement: the 44px close button, with aria-label="Close".
.modal__bodyElement: the content. Scrolls when long.
.modal__footerElement: the actions, right-aligned. Often a form method="dialog".
.modal-overlayThe :target wrapper for the CSS-only version.

Microcopy

  • Ask the question in the title: "Delete invoice 1042?"
  • Name both outcomes on the buttons: "Keep invoice", "Delete invoice". Not "Cancel" and "OK".

Helpers

Small classes for text that only screen readers need, accent borders and section spacing.

File: css/utilities/Helpers.css

Visually hidden

Hidden on screen, still read aloud. Use it for extra context on a short link, or a legend the design does not show.

View order 1042 Skip to content
Code 
<a href="organism-tables.html">View order<span class="visually-hidden"> 1042</span></a>
<a class="visually-hidden visually-hidden--focusable" href="#main">Skip to content</a>

Borders and sections

A quote or a callout, with a left accent.

A section with even space above and below.

Code 
<p class="border_left">A quote or a callout, with a left accent.</p>
<hr class="border-hr">
<section class="section"><p>A section with even space above and below.</p></section>

Classes

ClassUse
.visually-hiddenScreen-reader-only text.
.visually-hidden--focusableShows when focused, for skip links.
.border_left, _right, _top, _bottomA 6px accent border on one side.
.border-hrA short rounded divider.
.section30px of space above and below.
.demo_product, .demo_codeTinted boxes for showing components, as on these pages.

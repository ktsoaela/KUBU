Date Picker

Prefer the browser's own date input: it has a calendar, keyboard support and the visitor's date format.

File: css/organisms/DatePicker.css

Native date input (recommended)

Code 
<div class="form-group datepicker">
    <label class="form-label" for="dp-date">Delivery date</label>
    <input class="datepicker__input" id="dp-date" name="date" type="date" min="2026-11-02" max="2026-12-18" value="2026-11-11">
</div>

A month on the page

When people need to see the whole month, for example to spot free days, render the calendar on the server. <details> opens and closes it, and each day is a link the server can read.

            <caption class="visually-hidden">November 2026</caption>
            <thead><tr><th scope="col" abbr="Monday">Mo</th><th scope="col" abbr="Tuesday">Tu</th><th scope="col" abbr="Wednesday">We</th><th scope="col" abbr="Thursday">Th</th><th scope="col" abbr="Friday">Fr</th><th scope="col" abbr="Saturday">Sa</th><th scope="col" abbr="Sunday">Su</th></tr></thead>

        </table>
    </div>
</details>

The table is cut to three weeks here to keep the sample short.

Parts

ClassRole
.datepickerBlock: positions the calendar. A <details> for the open state.
.datepicker__inputElement: the field, or the summary showing the chosen date.
.datepicker__calendarElement: the month panel. Shown when the details is open.
.datepicker__headerElement: month name and the previous and next links.
td.today, td.selected, td.disabledDay states. The chosen day also has aria-current="date".

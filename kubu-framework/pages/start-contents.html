Component menu

Contents

Every KUBU component with one line on what it is for. Pick the one that matches what you need to build.

Atoms

Reset
Page colours, the focus ring and reduced motion. css/atoms/Reset.css
Avatar
A photo or initials for a person or a shop. css/atoms/Avatar.css
Badge
A short label for a count or a status. css/atoms/Badge.css
Buttons
Actions in every state: hover, active, disabled and loading. css/atoms/Buttons.css
Close
The labelled close control for dialogs, panels and alerts. css/atoms/Close.css
Colors
The semantic tokens a theme overrides. css/atoms/colors.css
Spacing
The --space-* scale and the margin and padding helpers. css/atoms/spacing.css
Spinners
Small loading indicators for a control or a region. css/atoms/Spinners.css
Typography
The type scale from metadata to the page title. css/atoms/typography.css

Molecules

Announcements
A one-line message across the top of the site. css/molecules/Announcements.css
Breadcrumb
Where this page sits in the site. css/molecules/Breadcrumb.css
Button Groups
Related actions joined into one control. css/molecules/ButtonGroups.css
Dropdowns
A menu that opens from a labelled button, no script. css/molecules/Dropdowns.css
Form Groups
A label, a field, a hint and an error, kept together. css/molecules/FormGroups.css
List Groups
Rows with an icon, a title and a line of text. css/molecules/ListGroups.css
Navbar
The labelled row of links in a header. css/molecules/Navbar.css
Navs
Links between views of one section: tabs, pills or a stack. css/molecules/Navs.css
Pagination
Links to the pages of a long list. css/molecules/Pagination.css

Organisms

Accordion
Stacked sections that open one at a time, or each on its own. css/organisms/Accordion.css
Alerts
A message with a tone, an icon and a sentence. css/organisms/Alerts.css
Card
A header, body and footer around one subject. css/organisms/Card.css
Carousel
Slides that snap into place as you scroll. css/organisms/Carousel.css
Collapse
Show and hide one region with <details>. css/organisms/Collapse.css
Date Picker
Choose a date with the native input, or show a month. css/organisms/DatePicker.css
File Upload
A labelled file field that looks like a button. css/organisms/FileUpload.css
Forms
Forgiving validation with :user-invalid and inline errors. css/organisms/Forms.css
List Groups
A panel of selectable rows, like a settings menu. css/organisms/ListGroups.css
Modal
A native <dialog> built from header, body and footer. css/organisms/Modal.css
Offcanvas
A panel that slides in from the edge. css/organisms/Offcanvas.css
Popovers
Extra content on demand with the popover attribute. css/organisms/Popovers.css
Progress
How far a task has got. css/organisms/Progress.css
Scrollspy
A sticky in-page menu that marks the section you jumped to. css/organisms/Scrollspy.css
Stepper
The steps of a process and where you are. css/organisms/Stepper.css
Tables
Data tables with captions, stripes and alignment. css/organisms/Tables.css
Tabs
A tab bar that switches panels with radio buttons. css/organisms/Tabs.css
Timeline
What happened, in order, with a date on each step. css/organisms/Timeline.css
Toasts
Short confirmations stacked in a corner. css/organisms/Toasts.css
Tooltips
A short text hint on hover and keyboard focus. css/organisms/Tooltips.css
Rating
A star rating made of radio buttons. css/organisms/Rating.css

Utilities

Ajax
Loading, skeleton and swap states for htmx requests. css/utilities/Ajax.css
Animations
Fade and slide in with transform and opacity. css/utilities/Animations.css
Event Delegation
One htmx listener on a parent instead of one per child.
Helpers
Visually hidden text, borders and section spacing. css/utilities/Helpers.css
Notifications
Inline status messages for a region. css/utilities/Notifications.css
Flexbox
The sidebar, main and footer page layout. css/utilities/Flexbox.css

Pages

Home Page
A product home page from the header, card and footer snippets.
About Page
A team and history page from cards, a list group and a table.
Product Page
A product page with a carousel, tabs, rating and a buy form.
Contact Page
A contact form with forgiving validation.

Snippets

Forms
Base styles for bare form elements. css/snippets/Forms.css
Navbar
The site header: logo, labelled menu and a utility row. css/snippets/Header.css
Footer
Footer links and the copyright line. css/snippets/Footer.css
Dock
The navbar at the bottom of a phone screen. css/snippets/Dock.css

Templates

Main Template
A full page layout: header, breadcrumb, content, footer.

List Groups

Rows of related items, each with an icon, a title and one line of text.

File: css/molecules/ListGroups.css. For selectable rows, see the ListGroups organism.

Example

  • Order confirmed

    Sent to lerato@example.com at 09:14.

  • Parcel shipped

    Tracking number LS-20419.

  • Delivered

    Signed for by the front desk.

Code 
<ul class="list-group">
    <li class="list-group-item">
        <svg class="icon icon--lg list-group-icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="../img/icons.svg#mail"></use></svg>
        <div class="list-group-content">
            <p class="list-group-title">Order confirmed</p>
            <p class="list-group-text">Sent to lerato@example.com at 09:14.</p>
        </div>
    </li>
    <li class="list-group-item">
        <svg class="icon icon--lg list-group-icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="../img/icons.svg#upload"></use></svg>
        <div class="list-group-content">
            <p class="list-group-title">Parcel shipped</p>
            <p class="list-group-text">Tracking number LS-20419.</p>
        </div>
    </li>
    <li class="list-group-item">
        <svg class="icon icon--lg list-group-icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="../img/icons.svg#check"></use></svg>
        <div class="list-group-content">
            <p class="list-group-title">Delivered</p>
            <p class="list-group-text">Signed for by the front desk.</p>
        </div>
    </li>
</ul>

Parts

ClassRole
ul.list-groupBlock: the list.
.list-group-itemOne row, with a divider below.
.list-group-iconThe leading icon, decorative.
.list-group-content, .list-group-title, .list-group-textThe text block, its title and its muted line.

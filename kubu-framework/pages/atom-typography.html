Typography

A type scale with one display size, the title. Headings use the body face and step down to body size, so the title is the one thing that reads first.

File: css/atoms/typography.css

The scale

Title, display face

Heading 2, body face at 20px

Heading 3, body face at 16px

Body text is 16px with a 1.75 line height, for comfortable reading.

Metadata, 12px and muted: updated 31 October 2026

Code 
<p class="title">Title, display face</p>
<h2>Heading 2, body face at 20px</h2>
<h3>Heading 3, body face at 16px</h3>
<p>Body text is 16px with a 1.75 line height, for comfortable reading.</p>
<p><small>Metadata, 12px and muted: updated 31 October 2026</small></p>

Keys and dividers

Write a key with <kbd>. Break a page into parts with <hr>, a line in the border colour. Neither needs a class.

Press Ctrl + K to search the shop, or Esc to close the results.

Orders placed after 14:00 leave the next working day.

Code 
<p>Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> to search the shop, or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close the results.</p>
<hr>
<p>Orders placed after 14:00 leave the next working day.</p>

Tokens

TokenValueUse
--font-size-xs0.75remBadges, metadata
--font-size-sm0.875remSecondary text, tables
--font-size-md1remBody
--font-size-lg1.25remLead text, .subtitle, h1 and h2
--font-size-display2.25rem to 3.25rem, by width.title, the only display size

Faces: --font-display (Raleway) sets .title and the logo only. --font-body (the system face) sets everything else, headings included. --font-family-mono is for code. Weights and line heights have tokens too.

Rules

  • One h1 per page, then headings in order.
  • One focal headline, one supporting sentence, then the content.

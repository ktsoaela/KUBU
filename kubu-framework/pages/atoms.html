Atoms of Kubu

Atoms are the smallest building blocks of a design system. They represent the foundational elements such as buttons, typography, colors, and spacing. These elements are combined to create more complex components (molecules and organisms) in the Atomic Design methodology.

How BEM and Atomic Design Work Together

BEM (Block, Element, Modifier) is a naming convention that helps create reusable and maintainable CSS. When combined with Atomic Design, BEM ensures that each atom, molecule, and organism is clearly defined and easy to understand. For example, a button (an atom) can be styled using BEM classes like .button (block), .button__icon (element), and .button--primary (modifier).

Atoms Overview

Below is a detailed breakdown of the atoms used in Kubu:

Reset

The reset atom ensures consistent styling across all browsers by removing default margins, paddings, and other browser-specific styles. It acts as a clean slate for your design system.

Badge

Badges are small, visual indicators used to highlight status, notifications, or counts. They are often used in navigation bars, buttons, or cards.

Buttons

Buttons are interactive elements that allow users to perform actions. They come in various styles, such as primary, secondary, and outline, and can include icons or text.

Close

The close atom is used to dismiss or close components like modals, alerts, or notifications. It typically appears as an "X" icon.

Colors

Colors define the visual identity of your design system. They include primary, secondary, and accent colors, as well as semantic colors for success, warning, and error states.

Spacing

Spacing atoms define the consistent use of margins and paddings throughout the design system. They ensure a balanced and harmonious layout.

Spinners

Spinners are used to indicate loading states. They provide visual feedback to users while content is being fetched or processed.

Typography

Typography atoms define the font families, sizes, weights, and line heights used in the design system. They ensure readability and consistency across all text elements.

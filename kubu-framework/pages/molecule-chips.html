Chips

Filter chips are radios or checkboxes whose labels are the chips. The input is clipped, not removed, so it stays in the tab order and the form posts the choice without a script.

File: css/molecules/Chips.css

Pick one, with All as the reset

Radios give one choice. The "All" chip is the reset. A parent reads the choice with :has(), so this list filters with no script.

Blanket weight
  • Seanamarena Victoria, 1.6 kg
  • Seanamarena Lekhotla, 2.4 kg
  • Poone, 1.5 kg
  • Morena, 2.6 kg
Code 
<form class="chips-demo" action="molecule-chips.html" method="get">
    <fieldset class="chips">
        <legend class="visually-hidden">Blanket weight</legend>
        <label class="chips__chip">All<input class="chips__input" type="radio" name="weight" value="all" checked></label>
        <label class="chips__chip">Light<input class="chips__input" type="radio" name="weight" value="light"></label>
        <label class="chips__chip">Heavy<input class="chips__input" type="radio" name="weight" value="heavy"></label>
    </fieldset>
    <ul class="chips-demo__list">
        <li data-weight="light">Seanamarena Victoria, 1.6 kg</li>
        <li data-weight="heavy">Seanamarena Lekhotla, 2.4 kg</li>
        <li data-weight="light">Poone, 1.5 kg</li>
        <li data-weight="heavy">Morena, 2.6 kg</li>
    </ul>
</form>

    </fieldset>
</form>

Parts

ClassRole
.chipsBlock: a <fieldset> of chips that wraps. Give it a <legend>, hidden if the page already says what it filters.
.chips__chipElement: the label, a 44px chip with the one radius and border.
.chips__inputElement: the radio or checkbox, clipped but focusable.
:checkedState: the chip turns over and its text goes bold.

Accessibility

  • Radios move with the arrow keys; checkboxes each take a Tab stop and toggle with Space.
  • The chip's word is its name. Do not use a colour swatch alone; add an aria-label if you must.
  • Say how many results are left in text next to the chips, as the Use Cases catalog does.

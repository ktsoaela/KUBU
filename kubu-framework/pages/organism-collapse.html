Collapse

Show and hide one region. <details> gives you the toggle, the keyboard support and the open state.

File: css/organisms/Collapse.css

Example

Show the delivery options
  • Collect from the Maseru depot: free
  • Courier to your door: R 85
Code 
<details class="collapse-details">
    <summary>Show the delivery options</summary>
    <ul>
        <li>Collect from the Maseru depot: free</li>
        <li>Courier to your door: R 85</li>
    </ul>
</details>

Secondary fields in a form

Progressive disclosure: keep optional fields closed until someone needs them.

Add a company name and VAT number
Code 
<form class="form-container doc-stack" action="organism-collapse.html" method="get">
    <div class="form-group">
        <label class="form-label" for="col-email">Email <span class="form-label__hint">(required)</span></label>
        <input class="form-input" id="col-email" name="email" type="email" autocomplete="email" required>
    </div>
    <details class="collapse-details">
        <summary>Add a company name and VAT number</summary>
        <div class="form-group">
            <label class="form-label" for="col-company">Company</label>
            <input class="form-input" id="col-company" name="company" type="text" autocomplete="organization">
        </div>
        <div class="form-group">
            <label class="form-label" for="col-vat">VAT number</label>
            <input class="form-input" id="col-vat" name="vat" type="text" placeholder="e.g. 4012345678">
        </div>
    </details>
    <button class="button button--primary" type="submit">Create account</button>
</form>

Parts

ClassRole
details.collapse-detailsBlock: the bordered disclosure.
summaryThe 44px toggle. Write what opening it shows.
Other childrenThe content, padded to match.
.collapse, .collapse__header, .collapse__bodyOlder markup for the KUBU 2.0 script, which toggles .open.

Spinners

A small loading indicator for one control or one region. For a region that is waiting on content, prefer a skeleton.

File: css/atoms/Spinners.css

With htmx

Give the spinner .htmx-indicator. It stays hidden until htmx marks the request as running.

Code 
<button class="button button--outline" type="button">Load more orders <span class="spinner spinner--small htmx-indicator" aria-hidden="true"></span></button>

Parts

ClassRole
.spinnerBlock: a ring. --spinner-size and --spinner-color tune it.
.spinner--small, --large, --secondary, --highlightModifier: size and colour.
.spinner-dual-ring, .spinner-dotsAlternative shapes.

Accessibility

  • A spinner that stands alone needs role="status" and hidden text, like "Loading orders".
  • A spinner inside a labelled button is decoration: aria-hidden="true", and aria-busy="true" on the button.
  • With reduced motion the rotation stops; the ring still shows.

2.0

31 October 2026

Current
  • One stylesheet, kubu.min.css, for the whole page.
  • Named parts: a card is a header, a body, and a footer.
  • The layout still works when the script is gone. htmx only swaps a region.
  • Dark is the master theme. Light is the pair, through light-dark().
  • A 12-column grid, and a 13-column grid when you need 5 and 8.
  • Packages for a starter site, icons, and type. The same CSS file in each.

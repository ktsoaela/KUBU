What changed in each KUBU release, newest first.

KUBU 2.0

Current

31 October 2026

  • Dark is the master theme. Each token has a dark value and a light pair, picked with light-dark().
  • A theme switch (Auto, Dark, Light) that works as plain radio buttons.
  • Every control is at least 44 by 44 pixels and shows a focus ring.
  • Buttons gained active and loading states; form errors gained an icon and a sentence.
  • Dropdowns, tabs, modals, offcanvas and the date picker work without JavaScript.
  • A 12-column grid, a scroll-snap carousel and skeletons for htmx regions.
  • The docs are one htmx site: each address is a real page, and only the content swaps.
  • node scripts/build.mjs bundles the CSS, or just the files you list.

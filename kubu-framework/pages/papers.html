Design rules

Every page in these papers follows the same rules. They are the acceptance criteria for new components.

  1. Real elements and landmarks: button, a, dialog, details, one h1.
  2. BEM matches the DOM: .block, .block__element, .block--modifier.
  3. Targets are at least 44 by 44 pixels, and every control shows a focus ring.
  4. Colour is never the only signal: errors carry an icon and a sentence.
  5. Motion is CSS only, transform and opacity, and stops for reduced motion.
  6. Dark is the master theme; with no saved choice the page follows the system.
